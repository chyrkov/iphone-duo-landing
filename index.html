    Tech specs

    iPhone Duo at a glance.

    Displays
    7.6″ foldable Super Retina XDR inside, nano-texture finish, wide-angle OLEDs. 5.4″ Super Retina XDR outside. Same aspect ratio. ProMotion up to 120Hz, Always-On, 3000 nits peak outdoor.
    Chip
    A20 Pro (2nm). 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, Dual 16-core Neural Engine. Custom vapor chamber.
    Cameras
    48MP Fusion Main with sensor-shift OIS and zero shutter lag. 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide with macro. 12MP Center Stage front camera. Under-display FaceTime camera. 4K 120 fps Dolby Vision.
    Battery
    Dual-battery design, one in each half. Up to 31 h video (inner), 44 h (outer), 24 h mixed use. About 50% in 20 min wired with a 60W-capable adapter (sold separately); about 30 min with MagSafe or Qi2.

    Touch ID in the side button. Camera Control with Siri mode in Camera.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is iPhone Duo a foldable or a flip?

    It’s a book-style foldable. Closed, it’s a 5.4-inch iPhone. Open, it’s a 7.6-inch display, the largest ever on an iPhone.

    Will I see a crease?

    The display is built to keep the fold flat and smooth. Custom adhesives let its layers glide like the pages of a book, and the hinge supports the centre of the display.

    Does it work with Apple Pencil?

    Apple Pencil (USB-C) support arrives later this year. Hover isn’t supported.

    Does it have a SIM tray?

    No. iPhone Duo is eSIM only, worldwide.

    Face ID or Touch ID?

    Touch ID, built into the side button.

    How durable is it?

    A Grade 5 titanium frame, a hinge of more than 100 components with a magnet array, Ceramic Shield 2 on the outer display, Ceramic Shield on the back, and IP68 water and dust resistance.
